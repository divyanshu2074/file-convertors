Frequently Asked Questions

Are my PDF documents uploaded to an external server?

No. Unlike traditional tools such as iLovePDF or Smallpdf, DocuZenith runs 100% client-side in your web browser using WebAssembly. Your files are processed entirely in your computer’s local memory and are never uploaded to any server.

Is DocuZenith HIPAA and GDPR compliant for sensitive legal and medical documents?

Yes. Because zero bytes of document data leave your device or enter cloud pipelines, DocuZenith inherently complies with strict confidentiality regulations, NDAs, GDPR, and HIPAA data residency rules.

Can I merge large PDFs without file size limits?

Yes! Because processing happens in your browser’s local RAM, there are no artificial file size limits, paywalls, or rate throttles.
